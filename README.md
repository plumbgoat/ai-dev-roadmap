# The AI-Augmented Developer Skill Tree

**Level up from "I use AI sometimes" to building AI into production.**

A gamified skill tree of **27 skills across 6 tiers** — from how the model
works under the hood to shipping AI in real products. Learn a skill, earn XP,
climb the ranks. Every skill links to a hand-picked course, paper, or doc.

🌳 **[Open the skill tree →](https://plumbgoat.github.io/ai-dev-roadmap/)**

## What's inside

Six tiers, each a level:

1. **Foundations** — how the model actually works (transformers, tokens, embeddings)
2. **Prompting** — fundamentals, advanced patterns, structured output, context engineering
3. **Code with AI** — pick your tool, work in the loop, debug, keep your edge
4. **Extend your agent** — MCP, skills & plugins, agents, hooks
5. **Build into products** — the API, RAG, tool use, evals & observability
6. **Production & frontier** — cost & scaling, security, multimodal, staying current

## Two ways to explore

- **🌳 Skill tree** — click an orb to mark a skill learned; earn XP, watch your
  rank evolve from *Prompt-Curious* to *Full-Stack AI Wizard*. Progress is
  saved in your browser.
- **🧠 Mind map** — the same 27 skills as an interactive branching map; click a
  branch to explore its skills.

## Resources

Every skill links out to real, well-known resources — Karpathy, 3Blue1Brown,
DeepLearning.AI, Anthropic docs, the canonical papers (*Attention Is All You
Need*, *ReAct*, *LoRA*), Hugging Face, OWASP, and more. They're curated
starting points, not an exhaustive syllabus. Skills marked ◆ link to small
tools by [plumbgoat](https://github.com/plumbgoat).
