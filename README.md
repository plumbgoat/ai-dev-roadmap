# The AI-Augmented Developer

**Level up from "I use AI sometimes" to building AI into production.**

A structured skill tree of **36 skills across 6 tiers** — from how the model
works under the hood to shipping AI in real products. Every skill has why it
matters, a "you've got it when" mastery check, the pitfall to dodge, and
hand-picked resources — and every tier ends in a hands-on project you
actually build.

🌳 **[Open it →](https://plumbgoat.github.io/ai-dev-roadmap/)** — one page, three views: a structured course curriculum, a self-directed skill tree, and a mind map.

## What's inside

Six tiers, each a level, each ending in a project:

1. **Foundations** (7) — how the model works: LLM basics, transformers & attention, training (base/instruct/RLHF), tokens & embeddings, reasoning models, open/local models, failure modes
2. **Prompting** (5) — fundamentals, system prompts & personas, advanced patterns, structured output, context engineering
3. **Code with AI** (6) — pick your driver, work in the loop, debug, test AI code, review like a stranger's PR, keep your edge
4. **Extend your agent** (6) — MCP, skills & plugins, build & ship a plugin, agents & orchestration, memory, hooks
5. **Build into products** (7) — the API, model choice, streaming & UX, RAG, tool use, fine-tune vs prompt vs RAG, evals & observability
6. **Production & frontier** (5) — cost & scaling, security, multimodal, responsible AI, staying current

## Three ways to explore

A switcher at the top of the page moves between all three — same 36 skills
underneath, same saved progress, different framing:

- **📘 Curriculum** — the two-track syllabus: Foundations Track (no coding
  background) and Builder Track (working developers), same six tiers at
  different depth and pace.
- **🌳 Skill tree** — click an orb to mark a skill learned and track your
  progress through the six tiers, at your own pace, in any order.
- **🧠 Mind map** — the same 36 skills as an interactive branching map; click a
  branch to explore its skills, and click a skill to mark it learned.

## Resources

Every skill links out to real, well-known resources — Karpathy, 3Blue1Brown,
DeepLearning.AI, Anthropic docs, the canonical papers (*Attention Is All You
Need*, *ReAct*, *LoRA*), Hugging Face, OWASP, and more. They're curated
starting points, not an exhaustive syllabus. Skills marked ◆ link to small
tools by [plumbgoat](https://github.com/plumbgoat).

## Contributing

Contributions are very welcome — especially content (a missing skill, a sharper
takeaway, a better link). It's one dependency-free HTML file, so there's no build
step.
